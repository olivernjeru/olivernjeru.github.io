House rules

Privacy, plainly stated.

Last updated: 16 May 2026

This site is the personal portfolio of Oliver Njeru. It runs no analytics, no third-party scripts, no advertising trackers, and no fingerprinting.

What is stored on your device

  • Theme preference. Light or dark mode is saved to your browser’s localStorage under the key hearthTheme. The value never leaves your device.

External links

Links to GitHub, LinkedIn, Google Scholar, Unsplash, Figma, and other sites open with rel="noopener noreferrer" so those destinations cannot read this site’s context. Their own privacy policies apply once you click through.

Fonts

Newsreader, Inter Tight, and JetBrains Mono are bundled with the site and served from the same origin (no third-party font CDN, no cookies).

Hosting

Served as static files from GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect server access logs as described in GitHub’s privacy statement.

Contact
